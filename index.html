About Laura

Hi, I'm Laura McDonald

#bikramyoga #yinyoga ☯

I'm a certified yoga teacher based in London, UK, teaching since 2009. I started teaching online so that anyone, anywhere, could roll out a mat and take a moment for themselves without worrying about traffic, childcare, or the weather.

My classes blend the discipline and heat of Bikram yoga with the slow, meditative depth of Yin — expect a calm, friendly space where you're encouraged to listen to your own body, not chase a perfect pose.

  • Certified Yoga Teacher since 2009 (17+ years experience)
  • Based in London, UK
  • Specialising in Bikram & Yin Yoga
